Stroll v0.3.0 Windows preview

Download Stroll-v0.3.0-Windows-portable.zip below (not GitHub's Source code ZIP),
extract it to a permanent folder on your laptop, then launch Stroll.exe. Keep
Stroll.exe.config beside it. Exit v2 from its tray menu before starting v3.

V3 adds J-Yosef to the built-in pets, English and Cantonese (Hong Kong) UI,
and a #F2EAAA default background with a reset button. Mudkip and Dorae are not
bundled because of character-rights concerns. No installer, account, network
requests or third-party application packages.

This is an unsigned preview. Start-menu listing/pinning remains unresolved in
one tested desktop session; the Add-Stroll-to-Start.cmd helper runs registration
in your normal Windows session. Physical sleep/wake and mixed-DPI checks remain.
Your desktop's imported pets and settings are not included or synced to the laptop.
